Ring Breaker

Three banks each see a few odd transfers. Together they see a laundering ring.

Each bank's AI agent works only on its own data. When a customer shows objective red flags, the agent asks the other banks narrow questions over Google's Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol, sharing masked IDs only. A policy agent checks every message.

    Alone vs together

    Measured by the evaluation harness (python -m ringbreaker.eval). A ring counts as found only if one group holds at least half of its accounts and is mostly that ring, so one giant blob can't take credit for many rings.

    Sharing rules

    Modelled on Singapore's COSMIC principle: banks share only when objective indicators of suspicion are present. The policy agent enforces these rules on every request and answer. Not legal advice.
